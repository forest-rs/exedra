# Mixed-depth adaptive dual contour extraction

Make exedra_isosurface dual contouring use intersecting leaves at their actual octree depth instead of forcing all extraction to the max-depth lattice.

## Design

Keep ownership local to exedra_isosurface. Reuse exedra_spatial leaf bounds and face-neighbor queries. Phase this as true mixed-depth leaf retention plus deterministic transition stitching; do not take on full manifold DC or new field semantics here.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. Intersecting octree leaves can emit dual vertices at their actual depth rather than only at `max_depth`.
2. Mixed-depth neighboring leaves stitch deterministically across depth transitions without invalid mesh topology.
3. Stats/docs describe the extractor as genuinely adaptive, not only interval-pruned.
4. Regression tests cover at least one shape whose extracted active cells span multiple depths.
5. `typos`, `cargo fmt --all`, `cargo test -p exedra_isosurface`, `cargo clippy -p exedra_isosurface --all-targets --all-features -- -D warnings`, and `cargo doc -p exedra_isosurface --no-deps` pass.

## Notes

**2026-03-24T06:16:13Z**

Implemented a conservative mixed-depth follow-up for phase-1 dual contouring. Active leaves now solve at their actual octree depth after a small minimum refinement floor, with a simple sign-pattern heuristic deciding when cells may stop early. Face emission still traverses the finest sign lattice, but it reuses coarse leaf vertices across their covered finest cells and suppresses duplicate or over-subscribed triangles with a deterministic undirected-edge incidence guard so the resulting mesh stays valid. Added a multiscale regression proving active leaves can span multiple depths and still validate.
