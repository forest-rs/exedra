Implement a debug/inspect operator that runs Exedra validation and wraps results into an OpReport with diagnostics. Exercises the diagnostics and reporting path.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Runs Exedra validate_fast/validate_deep
- Wraps validation errors into Cambium Diagnostics
- Produces OpReport with stats
- Unit tests with valid and invalid meshes
