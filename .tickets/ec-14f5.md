# Implement deterministic constructive stretch

Evaluate NodeKind::Stretch as an insert/remove-material modeling operation for exact constructive forms and closed imported meshes. The negative plane half-space remains rigid; the positive half-space moves along the normalized plane normal.

## Design

Define normalized-plane orientation and nested-node coordinate composition in a crate ADR. Prefer exact recipe/profile rewrites for boxes and extrusions; use a deterministic plane-section, band, and stitch path for general closed meshes. Preserve face regions and source features, diagnose unsupported topology under eval.stretch.*, and leave multi-zone policy to frontends composing single-plane nodes.

## Acceptance Criteria

Exact box and extrusion cases are resolution-independent; closed MeshImport stretch works; contraction removes a slab or refuses without folding; missing/tangent/coplanar/open/non-manifold cases have specified outcomes; nested stretches and CSG composition are tested; every output face has provenance and correct regions; AABBs and identical-input determinism are pinned; GeometryReport exposes stretch counters and typed diagnostics; a constructive wind-tunnel scenario measures exact and mesh paths; docs, ADR, and full workspace checks pass.
